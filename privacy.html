01

Information We Collect

Feathoria does not require visitors to create an account to browse its publicly available content. However, certain information may be collected when you interact with our website.

Information You Provide

If you contact us through our contact page, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • The contents of your message.
  • Any additional information you choose to share.

This information is used to receive and respond to your communication.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit Feathoria, certain technical and usage information may be collected by analytics and advertising services, including:

  • Browser type and device information.
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Pages visited and interactions.
  • Referral sources and visit duration.
  • Cookies and similar identifiers.

The information collected depends on your browser settings and the services you use.

02

How We Use Information

Information collected through Feathoria and its integrated services may be used to:

  • Respond to messages and inquiries.
  • Understand website traffic and visitor behavior.
  • Improve website functionality and performance.
  • Identify technical issues and improve usability.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through our ordinary website features.

03

Google Analytics

Feathoria uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, approximate geographic information, device details, browser details, and interactions with website content.

This information helps us understand which pages are useful and how we can improve the overall experience.

Google processes analytics information according to its own privacy practices.

Learn more: Google's Privacy Policy .

04

Advertising and Google AdSense

Feathoria may display advertisements through Google AdSense to support the maintenance and development of the website.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and other technologies to display, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements.

Depending on your location, applicable consent choices, and advertising settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google may process information about your visits to this and other websites to provide advertising services.

For more information, visit:

  • How Google Uses Information for Advertising
  • Google My Ad Center
05

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device by websites and third-party services. They can help websites remember preferences, understand usage, and support advertising.

Feathoria and its integrated third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Website analytics.
  • Advertising and ad measurement.
  • Security and functionality.
  • Remembering certain preferences.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect how some third-party services function.

Where required by applicable law, relevant consent mechanisms will be used before setting non-essential cookies or technologies.

06

Creative Tools and User Content

Feathoria may provide creative tools that allow visitors to write, customize, and generate images containing their own poems, quotes, or text.

Our browser-based Quote Maker is designed to generate content within the visitor's browser. Text entered into the tool is used to create the requested output.

We do not intentionally collect or store the text entered into this tool through Feathoria's website infrastructure. However, browser extensions, third-party services, or other integrations may have their own data practices.

Please avoid entering passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information into public creative tools.

07

Third-Party Websites and Services

Feathoria may use or link to services provided by other organizations, including:

  • Google Analytics.
  • Google AdSense.
  • Google Fonts.
  • External websites linked from our pages.
  • Third-party services used to process contact messages.

These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

Feathoria does not control the privacy practices of external websites. We encourage visitors to review the policies of third-party services before using them.

08

Data Retention and Security

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Messages submitted through our contact page may be retained for as long as necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain relevant correspondence, or address legitimate operational needs.

We take reasonable measures to protect information within our control. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party service providers maintain their own retention and security practices.

09

Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where applicable.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.

You can also manage cookies through your browser and adjust available Google advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center.

To submit a privacy-related request, please contact us through our Contact Page.

We may need to verify your request before taking action.

10

Children's Privacy

Feathoria is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through our website without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

11

International Visitors

Feathoria may be accessed by visitors from different countries.

Third-party services integrated into the website may process information on servers located in countries other than your own.

Where applicable, such processing is subject to the relevant provider's privacy practices and applicable legal requirements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page, together with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
